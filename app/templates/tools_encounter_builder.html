Method reference and assumptions

XP Threshold Budget

Each character contributes a level-based threshold. Raw monster XP is filled toward the selected budget.

2014 Adjusted XP

Raw XP is multiplied according to monster count, with an additional adjustment for unusually small or large parties.

Story-First Benchmark

Combined monster CR is compared with one quarter of total party levels below level 5 or one half at level 5 and above.

Composition Templates

Profiles create a starting roster shape—solo, duo, mixed squad, patrol, or horde—then select monsters near a party-relative CR target.
